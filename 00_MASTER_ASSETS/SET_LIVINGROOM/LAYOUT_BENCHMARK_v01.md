## Architectural rules
1. ONE straight stair, 1.2 m wide, against the BACK wall, rising left→right. Bottom step starts right next to the 현관 (entrance) so people come in and either go up or step into the living room.
2. Stair top arrives at a 2F landing/hallway that runs along the back wall to the right, over the kitchen side. Landing is at least 1.2 m deep, flat, and as wide as the stair.
3. Railing: the stair handrail runs continuously up the stair and turns at the top, then carries on horizontally along the open edge of the 2F hallway. The hallway railing has NO baluster across the stair arrival (that is the opening). Same material everywhere (white oak cap + slim oak or black flat-bar balusters, evenly spaced).
4. 2F hallway: open side faces the camera with a railing, closed side = back wall with 3 room doors (2F housemates' rooms). The 3F is implied, not shown.
5. 현관: front door on the far LEFT wall (or left back corner), tiled floor ~15 cm lower than the oak living floor, with a clear step-up edge. Tall built-in shoe cabinet (신발장) along the 현관 wall right beside the door, with a few shoes on the tile only. No shoes or shoe cabinet in the living area.
6. Living: sofa center, facing camera, in front of the space under/near the stair; table behind or beside. Kitchen island far right, perpendicular or parallel to camera, so actors face the lens.
7. Camera: 3-camera sitcom front view from the missing 4th wall. Back wall + stair + 2F hallway all visible in one wide shot. Ceiling double height above the living area (open to below) so the 2F railing is seen from below.
