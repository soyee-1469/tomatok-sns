# VISUAL BIBLE — TOMATOK MULTICULTURAL SITCOM

## Format
- 기본: 9:16 vertical
- 필요 시 피드/광고 재가공: 4:5, 1:1

## Look
Warm contemporary family sitcom.
Natural, relatable, lightly humorous.
Realistic documentary-comedy tone.
Not a polished corporate commercial.

## Camera
- Master / Wide / Two Shot: 35mm 느낌
- Dialogue Medium: 50mm 느낌
- Reaction Close-up: 70–85mm 느낌
- 기본 높이: eye-level
- 대부분 locked-off
- 아주 미세한 handheld breathing 허용

## Shot grammar
대화 장면 기본 순서:
MASTER → SPEAKER A → SPEAKER B → REACTION → INSERT → MASTER

## Lighting
- soft natural window light
- warm practical household lighting
- natural skin tones
- low-to-medium contrast
- soft highlights

## Color
- cream white
- beige
- natural light wood
- muted neutral clothing
- TOMATOK red-orange accent under 5% of frame

## Performance
- small facial reactions
- realistic pauses
- no exaggerated sitcom acting
- no commercial smile
- camera를 보는 행동은 대본에 있을 때만

## Negative
- neon cyberpunk
- over-HDR
- orange skin
- teal-orange blockbuster grade
- aggressive camera moves
- ultra-wide facial distortion
- stock-photo posing
- random text
- fake UI
