# PROMPT MASTER

## 1. COMMON VISUAL BLOCK
Warm contemporary multicultural family sitcom, realistic, naturalistic, relatable and lightly humorous. Eye-level camera, soft natural window light, warm practical lighting, natural skin tones, low-to-medium contrast, cream white, beige and light wood palette, subtle TOMATOK warm red-orange accents only. No stock-photo posing, no exaggerated acting, no fake UI, no random letters, no neon cyberpunk, no oversaturated HDR.

## 2. CHARACTER BLOCK
Use the exact approved character reference. Preserve face identity, age, facial proportions, hairstyle, skin tone and body proportions. Do not reinterpret the character.

## 3. ENVIRONMENT BLOCK
Use the approved environment reference. Preserve room geometry, door/window positions, furniture layout, materials and established color palette.

## 4. CAMERA BLOCK
Choose only from established shot grammar:
- 35mm Master/Wide/Two Shot
- 50mm Dialogue Medium
- 70–85mm Reaction Close-up
Eye-level, mostly locked camera, subtle breathing only.
